01What services does Godhury Marketing provide?

We provide design and branding, printing solutions, outdoor advertising, signage, vehicle branding, event production, and custom exhibition stall support for businesses in Bangladesh.

02How do I request a quotation?

You can use the contact form, email us at sales@godhurymarketing.com, or call/WhatsApp +880 1715 053503. Sharing size, quantity, material, location, and deadline helps us prepare a faster estimate.

03Can you help with design if I only have an idea?

Yes. Our team can help turn your brief into brand visuals, print-ready artwork, signage concepts, campaign materials, or event production layouts.

04What artwork files do you need for printing?

Print-ready PDF, AI, EPS, or high-resolution PSD files are preferred. If you are unsure about format, send what you have and our production team will review it before final output.

05Do you manage billboard and outdoor advertising placement?

Yes. We support outdoor campaign planning, billboard production, transit branding, signage, installation coordination, and visibility-focused placement discussions based on campaign needs.

06How long does a typical project take?

Timelines depend on scope, material, approval speed, and installation needs. Small print jobs may move quickly, while outdoor, event, or custom fabrication projects need more planning and production time.

07Do you work outside Dhaka?

Yes. Godhury delivers projects across Bangladesh. Our head office and production facility are in Dhaka, while our project delivery and client service are nationwide.
